I am an MPhil student at HKUST(GZ), advised by Prof. Zeke Xie. My work focuses on generative AI, efficient sampling, visual foundation models, and data-centric model improvement.

Currently, I am a Research Intern at Alibaba Group's Qwen Image Foundation Model Team, where I contribute end-to-end to Qwen Image 2.0, Qwen Image 3.0, Turbo, RL, and few-step variants across data pipelines, training feedback, distillation, evaluation, and product-facing iteration.

## Research Thread

My research follows one practical question: how can we make visual generation models better, faster, and more reliable at scale?

| Direction | Focus | Representative works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sampling | Better generation trajectories and inference acceleration | Golden Noise, Zigzag Diffusion Sampling, IV-Mixed Sampler |
| Distillation | Compressing many-step generation into few-step models | Adaptive Matching Distillation, Trust-Weighted DMD, Qwen Image few-step variants |
| Data and Evaluation | Turning data, metrics, and user feedback into model improvement signals | D2C, Guidance Matters, LiveEditor, Qwen-Image-Bench |
| Industrial Foundation Models | End-to-end iteration of large visual generation systems | Qwen Image 2.0, Qwen Image 3.0, Turbo, Qwen-Image-2.0-RL, Qwen-Image-Agent |
